01 North Star Vision

THE DESTINATION

FixFlowAI becomes the revenue intelligence layer for AI-era service businesses — the system that knows what wins, auto-generates winning proposals, closes faster, and learns from every outcome across every agency on the platform.

What this means concretely in 3–5 years:

  • Not a proposal writer. Every competitor is a proposal writer. FixFlowAI is a deal prediction and optimization system that happens to generate proposals. The product tells you what to propose, how to price it, which risks will kill the deal, and when to follow up — because it has seen 100,000 outcomes.

  • Network effect locked. The platform's Agency Brain, calibrated across thousands of agencies with opt-in anonymous outcome data, creates the world's first proposal intelligence commons. The more agencies use it, the smarter every agency's proposals get. Impossible to replicate from scratch.

  • Client-side gravity. Clients who interact with FixFlowAI portals, sign proposals, leave feedback, and make decisions on the platform create a bilateral network. Agencies bring clients; clients expect FixFlowAI-grade proposals. A new pull-loop that Notion, Jasper, and Proposify cannot match.

  • Autonomous delivery. By year 3, the "generate proposal" step is a fallback. The system has enough agency context to proactively draft proposals for inbound briefs, notify the team, and request approval — not the other way around.

  • Vertical dominance, not horizontal sprawl. Own the full revenue cycle for service businesses: brief → proposal → negotiation → delivery → payment → reputation. Not a general AI assistant. Not a document generator. A specialized command center for selling professional services.

05 AI Architecture Evolution

FROM TOOL TO AUTONOMOUS SYSTEM

CURRENT Now
  • Single-shot generation: brief → Gemini → schema-validated JSON
  • Chat mutation: intent classification → section targeting → merge
  • Agency Brain: pattern extraction from historical proposals
  • Provider fallback: Gemini → OpenRouter → Ollama
PHASE 2 3–9m
  • Prediction injection: Agency Brain patterns injected into prompt context at generation time — not just calibration, but active priors
  • Multi-turn clarification agent: before generating, system asks 2–3 targeted questions based on brief gaps detected by BriefScore
  • Confidence calibration model: compare predicted confidence vs. actual win/loss outcomes, retrain the scoring prompt quarterly
  • Evaluation harness: automated proposal quality scoring against a rubric — run on every generated proposal, log results
PHASE 3 9–18m
  • Agentic proposal graph: Brief intake → BriefScore → Clarification → Research (web) → Generation → Review request → Client delivery — orchestrated as a named agent, no human required per step
  • Cross-agency intelligence: anonymized opt-in outcome data aggregated into a shared model; every agency's Agency Brain improves from the collective
  • Fine-tuned domain model: fine-tune Gemini (or equivalent) on your proprietary proposal corpus for superior JSON quality and domain accuracy
  • Delivery risk prediction: post-win, the system monitors delivery vs. proposal estimates and alerts when a project is deviating from its confidence profile
PHASE 4 18m+
  • Autonomous proposal agent: receives brief via email/webhook, drafts proposal, seeks internal approval, sends to client — fully unattended
  • Negotiation AI: during deal room phase, AI drafts counter-proposal language, suggests concessions with win-rate impact analysis
  • Revenue forecasting: predict deal close probability and revenue pipeline from current proposal portfolio

Critical AI architecture decision: Your current LLM layer is provider-agnostic (client.js with Gemini/OpenRouter/xAI/Ollama). Keep this. But add a dedicated eval harness that scores every generation against a quality rubric and logs to MongoDB. Without eval data, you cannot prove improvement. With eval data, you have a proprietary benchmark for proposal quality — a product story no competitor can match.

Target UX Model

Specific UX Transformations

  • Dashboard → Intelligence Hub. Replace the proposal list with a live revenue pulse: pipeline value, deals at risk, proposals overdue for follow-up, WinScore on pending deals, one-click AI-drafted follow-up messages. This makes opening FixFlowAI a daily habit, not a "when I need to write a proposal" event.

  • Brief intake → guided interview. Instead of "paste your brief," walk the user through 5 structured questions if no file is uploaded. BriefScore improves automatically because the intake is structured. Reduces garbage-in dramatically and is faster than writing a 500-word brief for most users.

  • Analytics → proactive briefing. The Analytics page is currently pull (you go look). Add a push layer: every Monday, a "Weekly Proposal Intelligence" email with 3 data-backed actions. This is retention engineering disguised as product value.
