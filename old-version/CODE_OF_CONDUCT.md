# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* **Be respectful and professional**: Avoid using offensive language, personal attacks, or exclusionary jokes.
* **Be collaborative**: We are a team. Help others, ask questions, and share knowledge.
* **Be constructive**: Provide actionable feedback during code reviews and accept constructive criticism gracefully.
* **Focus on what is best for the community**: Put the project and community first.
* **Gracefully accept constructive criticism**: Learn from feedback and use it to grow.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, fair, and corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project or its community in public spaces. Examples of representing our project or community include using an official email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

---

## Reporting & Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project administrators by contacting the core maintainers privately through their GitHub profiles or organization contacts.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All project leaders have a duty to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident. Corrective action will be taken as deemed necessary and appropriate by the project maintainers.
